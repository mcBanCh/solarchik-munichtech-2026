## Project name

Solarchik — a voice AI friend you carry everywhere

## Problem

Most apps are silent screens. Games do not talk back. Assistants sit in a separate chat and know nothing about what you are doing. People want one companion that stays with them in play and in real life: jokes during a run, listens when you speak, and can take a call when you cannot.

## Solution

Solarchik is a small solar robot friend that lives on your phone. In the room you talk to him by voice. In the runner he runs with you and answers only when you press the mic, so he does not interrupt. In the red phone booth he becomes a secretary: he takes the story of a call, writes who called, why, and what to do next, then speaks the report. One character, one voice, three modes — play, talk, handle calls.

## Tech stack

React, Gemini, OpenAI, Featherless, Android SpeechRecognizer, Web Speech API

## AI disclosure

Solarchik is a multimodal companion. Speech-to-text on device. Replies from Gemini, with OpenAI/Featherless as fallback. In-game he speaks short lines in the same voice as the room chat. The booth secretary turns a spoken or typed call into a structured report. The user starts every session. No medical claims.
